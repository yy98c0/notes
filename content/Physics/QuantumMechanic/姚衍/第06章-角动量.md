### 6.1 角动量之定义及其对易关系

> [!definition] 轨道角动量（经典力学）
> 在经典力学中，运动质点相对于参考原点之轨道角动量，定义为
>
> $\vec{\ell} = \vec{r} \times \vec{p} \quad (6.1)$

其中 $\vec{r}$ 为原点至质点之位置向量，$\vec{p}$ 为质点之线动量。$\vec{\ell}$ 在直角坐标系上之三个垂直分量，则分别为

$\ell_x = yp_z - zp_y, \quad \ell_y = zp_x - xp_z, \quad \ell_z = xp_y - yp_x \quad (6.2)$

在量子力学中，由公设二，轨道角动量及其分量则应表示成算符形式，即

$\hat{L} = \hat{R} \times \hat{P}, \quad \hat{L}_x = \hat{Y}\hat{P}_z - \hat{Z}\hat{P}_y, \quad \hat{L}_y = \hat{Z}\hat{P}_x - \hat{X}\hat{P}_z, \quad \hat{L}_z = \hat{X}\hat{P}_y - \hat{Y}\hat{P}_x \quad (6.3)$

由位置—动量关系式

$\left[ \hat{X}, \hat{P}_x \right] = i\hbar, \quad \left[ \hat{Y}, \hat{P}_y \right] = i\hbar, \quad \left[ \hat{Z}, \hat{P}_z \right] = i\hbar \quad (6.4)$

可得出角动量 $\hat{L}_x, \hat{L}_y, \hat{L}_z$ 之间的对易关系式，会有：

$$
\begin{align*} [\hat{L}_x, \hat{L}_y] &= \hat{L}_x \hat{L}_y - \hat{L}_y \hat{L}_x \\ &= (\hat{Y}\hat{P}_z - \hat{Z}\hat{P}_y) (\hat{Z}\hat{P}_x - \hat{X}\hat{P}_z) - (\hat{Z}\hat{P}_x - \hat{X}\hat{P}_z) (\hat{Y}\hat{P}_z - \hat{Z}\hat{P}_y) \\ &= \hat{P}_z \hat{Z} (\hat{Y}\hat{P}_x - \hat{X}\hat{P}_y) + \hat{Z}\hat{P}_z (\hat{P}_y \hat{X} - \hat{P}_x \hat{Y}) \\ &= (\hat{X}\hat{P}_y - \hat{Y}\hat{P}_x) (\hat{Z}\hat{P}_z - \hat{P}_z \hat{Z}) \\ &= i\hbar \hat{L}_z \end{align*} \quad (6.5)
$$

同理可得

$[\hat{L}_y, \hat{L}_z] = i\hbar \hat{L}_x, \quad [\hat{L}_z, \hat{L}_x] = i\hbar \hat{L}_y \quad (6.6)$

我们便利用此轨道角动量算符特殊之对易关系，来推广定义量子力学中的角动量。

> [!definition] 量子力学中的角动量
> 量子力学中之角动量 $\vec{J}$（不局限为轨道角动量 $\vec{\ell}$），是由三个可观测量或厄米算符 $\hat{J}_x, \hat{J}_y, \hat{J}_z$ 所组成。且它们彼此间须满足
>
> $[\hat{J}_x, \hat{J}_y] = i\hbar \hat{J}_z, \quad [\hat{J}_y, \hat{J}_z] = i\hbar \hat{J}_x, \quad [\hat{J}_z, \hat{J}_x] = i\hbar \hat{J}_y$
>
> 或
>
> $\hat{J} \times \hat{J} = i\hbar \hat{J}$
>
> 或 $[\hat{J}_\ell, \hat{J}_m] = i\hbar\epsilon_{\ell mk} \hat{J}_k$ 的循环关系式（cyclic relation）。

另外，当我们再引进一厄米算符 $\hat{J}^2$

$\hat{J}^2 = \hat{J}_x^2 + \hat{J}_y^2 + \hat{J}_z^2 \quad (6.7)$

则

$$
\begin{align*} [\hat{J}^2, \hat{J}_x] &= [\hat{J}_x^2, \hat{J}_x] + [\hat{J}_y^2, \hat{J}_x] + [\hat{J}_z^2, \hat{J}_x] \\ &= \hat{J}_y [\hat{J}_y, \hat{J}_x] + [\hat{J}_y, \hat{J}_x] \hat{J}_y + \hat{J}_z [\hat{J}_z, \hat{J}_x] + [\hat{J}_z, \hat{J}_x] \hat{J}_z \\ &= \hat{J}_y (-i\hbar \hat{J}_z) + (-i\hbar \hat{J}_z) \hat{J}_y + \hat{J}_z (i\hbar \hat{J}_y) + (i\hbar \hat{J}_y) \hat{J}_z = 0 \end{align*}
$$

同理

$[\hat{J}^2, \hat{J}_y] = 0, \quad [\hat{J}^2, \hat{J}_z] = 0 \quad (6.9)$

如此，若能得到愈多彼此可对易的算符关系，愈可以协助我们扩大和获得更多信息。量子力学中的角动量理论，可以完全由上述之对易关系建立起来。

由式(6.1)~(6.3)，我们无法同时去测量与获知，角动量三个分量之实验值，但由式(6.4)~(6.6)，却可以同时测量得 $\hat{J}^2$ 和 $\hat{J}_x$、$\hat{J}^2$ 和 $\hat{J}_y$，或 $\hat{J}^2$ 和 $\hat{J}_z$。在不失其普遍性下，一般皆选取 $\hat{J}^2$ 与 $\hat{J}_z$ 做为单一角动量之 C.S.C.O.。以后将陆续可看到，如何利用 $\hat{J}^2$ 和 $\hat{J}_z$ 来进行讨论。

From the definition of angular momentum $\mathbf{J}$ and its commutation relation with $\mathbf{J}^2$ , we have

$[J_{x},J_{y}] = i\hbar J_{z},\quad [J^{2},J_{i}] = 0$

where $i = x,y$ ,or $z$ .and

$J^{2} = J_{x}^{2} + J_{y}^{2} + J_{z}^{2}$

By the mathematical fundamental theorem II, there exist certain common eigenstates for both $J^2$ and $J_{z}$ , with corresponding eigenvalues, denoted by $\lambda$ and $m\hbar$ . Namely,

${\bf J}^{2}|\lambda m\rangle = \lambda |\lambda m\rangle$

$J_{z}|\lambda m\rangle = m\hbar |\lambda m\rangle$

Now, we may construct two conjugate operators for $J_{x}$ and $J_{y}$ as follows

$\boxed {J_{+} = J_{x} + iJ_{y},\qquad J_{-} = J_{x} - iJ_{y}}$

(1) Some important commutation relations of $J_{+}$ and $J_{-}$ with $J_{z}$ are easily evaluated:

$[J_{z},J_{+}] = \hbar J_{+}\qquad [J_{z},J_{-}] = -\hbar J_{-}\qquad [J_{+},J_{-}] = 2\hbar J_{z} \quad (1)$

Additionally,

$\underline{{J_{+}J_{-}}} = (J_{x} + iJ_{y})(J_{x} - iJ_{y}) = J_{x}^{2} + J_{y}^{2} + i(J_{y}J_{x} - J_{x}J_{y}) = \mathbf{J}^{2} - J_{z}^{2} + \hbar J_{z} \quad (2)$

Similarly,

$\underline{{J_{-}J_{+}}} = (J_{x} - iJ_{y})(J_{x} + iJ_{y}) = J_{x}^{2} + J_{y}^{2} - i(J_{y}J_{x} - J_{x}J_{y}) = \mathbf{J}^{2} - J_{z}^{2} - \hbar J_{z} \quad (3)$

(2) We will show that $J_{+}$ and $J_{-}$ are indeed raising and lowering operators. From eq (1), one has

$J_{z}J_{+} = J_{+}(J_{z} + \hbar)$

Hence,

$J_{z}J_{+}|\lambda m\rangle = J_{+}(J_{z} + \hbar)|\lambda m\rangle = (m + 1)\hbar J_{+}|\lambda m\rangle$

We can see that $J_{+}|\lambda m\rangle$ is an eigenstate of $J_{z}$ with the eigenvalue $(m + 1)\hbar$ . Hence $J_{+}$ is a raising operator; it raises the eigenvalue of $J_{z}$ by one unit of $\hbar$ . By the similar way,

$J_{z}J_{-}|\lambda m\rangle = J_{-}(J_{z} - \hbar)|\lambda m\rangle = (m - 1)\hbar J_{-}|\lambda m\rangle$

### 6.2 角动量的量子化及其观测值

> [!definition] 角动量的升降算符
> 现定义两个非常特殊的角动量之上升和下降(raising and lowering)算符：
>
> $\hat{J}_+ = \hat{J}_x + i \hat{J}_y, \quad \hat{J}_- = \hat{J}_x - i \hat{J}_y \quad (6.10)$

我们底下将仅使用 $\hat{J}^2$、$\hat{J}_z$与 $\hat{J}_+$、$\hat{J}_-$，来揭示角动量的本征值不可能连续，而必须为量子化值。此四算符彼此间，有下列常用之对易关系：

$$
\begin{aligned} [\hat{J}_z, \hat{J}_+] &= [\hat{J}_z, \hat{J}_x] + i[\hat{J}_z, \hat{J}_y] \\ &= i\hbar \hat{J}_y + i(-i\hbar \hat{J}_x) = \hbar(\hat{J}_x + i\hat{J}_y) = \hbar \hat{J}_+ \end{aligned} \quad (6.11)
$$

$\text{同理} \quad [\hat{J}_z, \hat{J}_-] = -\hbar \hat{J}_- \quad (6.12)$

$\text{又} \quad [\hat{J}^2, \hat{J}_\pm] = [\hat{J}^2, \hat{J}_x] \pm i[\hat{J}^2, \hat{J}_y] = 0 \quad (6.13)$

$\hat{J}_+ \hat{J}_- = (\hat{J}_x + i \hat{J}_y)(\hat{J}_x - i \hat{J}_y) = \hat{J}_x^2 + \hat{J}_y^2 - i[\hat{J}_x, \hat{J}_y] = \hat{J}^2 - \hat{J}_z^2 + \hbar \hat{J}_z \quad (6.14)$

$\text{同理} \quad \hat{J}_- \hat{J}_+ = \hat{J}^2 - \hat{J}_z^2 - \hbar \hat{J}_z \quad (6.15)$

综合上述，有

$[\hat{J}_z, \hat{J}_\pm] = \pm \hbar \hat{J}_\pm, \quad [\hat{J}^2, \hat{J}_\pm] = 0, \quad \hat{J}^2 = \hat{J}_\pm \hat{J}_\mp + \hat{J}_z^2 \mp \hbar \hat{J}_z \quad (6.16)$

利用这些关系，我们将可证明重要的角动量量子化定理。

> [!theorem] 定理6-1 角动量的量子化
> 若 $\hat{J}$ 为一角动量算符，即遵守
>
> $[\hat{J}_x, \hat{J}_y] = i\hbar \hat{J}_z, \quad [\hat{J}_y, \hat{J}_z] = i\hbar \hat{J}_x, \quad [\hat{J}_z, \hat{J}_x] = i\hbar \hat{J}_y$
>
> 之对易关系，则 $\hat{J}^2$ 和 $\hat{J}_z$ 的本征值将分别为 $j(j+1)\hbar^2$ 和 $m\hbar$，且
>
> (1) $j$ 值必为正整数、半整数或零，如：0, 1/2, 1, ... 等。
>
> (2) 若 $j$ 值确定，则 $m$ 值可有 $(2j+1)$ 个可能值，并为
>
> $-j, -j+1, \dots, j-1, j$
>
> 此外，$j$ 与 $m$ 值，必同为整数或半整数。

From the definition of angular momentum $\mathbf{J}$ and its commutation relation with $\mathbf{J}^2$ , we have

## Ch6-3 角动量的量子化

From the definition of angular momentum $\mathbf{J}$ and its commutation relation with $\mathbf{J}^2$ , we have

$[J_{x},J_{y}] = i\hbar J_{z},\quad [J^{2},J_{i}] = 0$

where $i = x,y$ , or $z$ . and

$J^{2} = J_{x}^{2} + J_{y}^{2} + J_{z}^{2}$

By the mathematical fundamental theorem II, there exist certain common eigenstates for both $J^2$ and $J_{z}$ , with corresponding eigenvalues, denoted by $\lambda$ and $m\hbar$ . Namely,

$J^{2}|\lambda m\rangle = \lambda |\lambda m\rangle$

$J_{z}|\lambda m\rangle = m\hbar |\lambda m\rangle$

Now, we may construct two conjugate operators for $J_{x}$ and $J_{y}$ as follows

$\boxed{J_{+} = J_{x} + iJ_{y},\qquad J_{-} = J_{x} - iJ_{y}}$

(1) Some important commutation relations of $J_{+}$ and $J_{-}$ with $J_{z}$ are easily evaluated:

$[J_{z},J_{+}] = \hbar J_{+}\qquad [J_{z},J_{-}] = -\hbar J_{-}\qquad [J_{+},J_{-}] = 2\hbar J_{z} \quad (1)$

Additionally,

$\underline{{J_{+}J_{-}}} = (J_{x} + iJ_{y})(J_{x} - iJ_{y}) = J_{x}^{2} + J_{y}^{2} + i(J_{y}J_{x} - J_{x}J_{y}) = \mathbf{J}^{2} - J_{z}^{2} + \hbar J_{z} \quad (2)$

Similarly,

$\underline{{J_{-}J_{+}}} = (J_{x} - iJ_{y})(J_{x} + iJ_{y}) = J_{x}^{2} + J_{y}^{2} - i(J_{y}J_{x} - J_{x}J_{y}) = \mathbf{J}^{2} - J_{z}^{2} - \hbar J_{z} \quad (3)$

(2) We will show that $J_{+}$ and $J_{-}$ are indeed raising and lowering operators. From eq (1), one has

$J_{z}J_{+} = J_{+}(J_{z} + \hbar)$

Hence,

$J_{z}J_{+}|\lambda m\rangle = J_{+}(J_{z} + \hbar)|\lambda m\rangle = (m + 1)\hbar J_{+}|\lambda m\rangle$

We can see that $J_{+}|\lambda m\rangle$ is an eigenstate of $J_{z}$ with the eigenvalue $(m + 1)\hbar$ . Hence $J_{+}$ is a raising operator; it raises the eigenvalue of $J_{z}$ by one unit of $\hbar$ . By the similar way,

$J_{z}J_{-}|\lambda m\rangle = J_{-}(J_{z} - \hbar)|\lambda m\rangle = (m - 1)\hbar J_{-}|\lambda m\rangle$

Thus $J_{- }$ is the lowering operator. When acting on an angular momentum eugenstate, it lowers the eigenvalue of $J_{z}$ by $\hbar$ .

(3) What are the eigenvalues for $\lambda$ and $m\hbar$ ? We first claim that $m$ is bounded. The mean value of $J_{x}^{2} + J_{y}^{2}$ in the state $|\lambda m\rangle$ is nonnegative. Consequently,

$0\leq \langle \lambda m|J_{x}^{2} + J_{y}^{2}|\lambda m\rangle = \langle \lambda m|\mathbf{J}^{2} - J_{z}^{2}|\lambda m\rangle = \lambda -(m\hbar)^{2}\quad \Rightarrow \lambda \geq (m\hbar)^{2}$

Therefore, for fixed $\lambda$ , the value of $m$ is bounded, and denote them $m_{max}$ and $m_{min}$ . Second, what are $m_{max}$ and $m_{min}$ ? Since the eigenvalues $m\hbar$ can be increased or decreased by the raising and lowering operators in discrete steps of $\hbar$ , $m$ must be discrete forming a ladder of unit steps, and $m_{min}$ is the lowest step of the ladder. The lowering operator cannot produce any effect, namely,

$\boxed{J_{-}|\lambda m_{min}\rangle = 0}$

Operating on this by $J_{+}$ , we reach

$J_{+}J_{-}|\lambda m_{min}\rangle = 0$

By eq (2), we get

$(\mathbf{J}^{2} - J_{z}^{2} + \hbar J_{z})|\lambda m_{min}\rangle = 0$

This gives

$\lambda -m_{min}(m_{min} - 1)\hbar^{2} = 0 \quad (4)$

(4) Similarly, there is a top step among the ladder of $J_{z}$ , say $m_{max}$ , such that

$\boxed{J_{+}|\lambda m_{max}\rangle = 0}$

Applying $J_{- }$ on this equation, and using $J_{-}J_{+} = \mathbf{J}^{2} - J_{z}^{2} - \hbar J_{z}$ , we obtain

$\lambda -m_{max}(m_{max} + 1)\hbar^{2} = 0 \quad (5)$

Combining eqs (4) and (5) gives

$m_{max}(m_{max} + 1) = m_{min}(m_{min} - 1)$

The reasonable solutions for the above equation is

$m_{max} = -m_{min} = j \quad (6)$

Substituting eq (6) into eqs (4) or (5), we have

$\boxed{\lambda = j(j + 1)\hbar^{2}}$

(5) Each time $J_{-}$ operates, the value of $J_{z}$ goes down by one unit of $\hbar$ . Namely, by applying $J_{-}$ on the state $|\lambda m_{max}\rangle$ for $n$ -times, we may reach $|\lambda m_{min}\rangle$ . It follows that the difference between $m_{max}$ and $m_{min}$ must be positive integer $n$ . Namely,

$m_{max} - n = m_{min}\quad \Rightarrow \quad m_{max} - m_{min} = n$

On the other hand, by eq (6),

$m_{max} - m_{min} = j - (-j) = 2j$

Hence,

$j = n / 2 = \mathrm{integer}\qquad \mathrm{if~n = even}$

$j = n / 2 = \mathrm{half - integer}\qquad \mathrm{if~n = odd}$

Finally, the eigenvalues of $\mathbf{J}^{2}$ are quantized, and the eigenvalues of $m$ are also quantized and satisfy

$\boxed{-j\leq m\leq j}$

The values of $m$ are integers if $j$ is integer, and half-integers if $j$ is half-integers, vice versa. We may summarize the results as follows

$\mathbf{J}^{2}|j m\rangle = j(j + 1)\hbar^{2}|j m\rangle$

$J_{z}|j m\rangle = m\hbar |j m\rangle$

where $m = -j, -j + 1, \dots, j - 1, j$ and $m$ should be either integers or half-integers at the same time.

## Ch6-4 角动量的矩阵形式

### 6.2 角动量的量子化及其观测值

> [!definition] 角动量的升降算符
> 现定义两个非常特殊的角动量之上升和下降 (raising and lowering) 算符：
>
> $\hat{J}_+ \equiv \hat{J}_x + i \hat{J}_y, \quad \hat{J}_- \equiv \hat{J}_x - i \hat{J}_y \quad (6.10)$

我们底下将仅使用 $\hat{J}^2$、$\hat{J}_z$ 与 $\hat{J}_+$、$\hat{J}_-$，来揭示角动量的本征值不可能连续，而必须为量子化值。此四算符彼此间，有下列常用之对易关系：

$$
\begin{aligned} [\hat{J}_z, \hat{J}_+] &= [\hat{J}_z, \hat{J}_x] + i[\hat{J}_z, \hat{J}_y] \\ &= i\hbar \hat{J}_y + i(-i\hbar \hat{J}_x) = \hbar(\hat{J}_x + i\hat{J}_y) = \hbar \hat{J}_+ \end{aligned} \quad (6.11)
$$

$\text{同理} \quad [\hat{J}_z, \hat{J}_-] = -\hbar \hat{J}_- \quad (6.12)$

$\text{又} \quad [\hat{J}^2, \hat{J}_\pm] = [\hat{J}^2, \hat{J}_x] \pm i[\hat{J}^2, \hat{J}_y] = 0 \quad (6.13)$

$\hat{J}_+ \hat{J}_- = (\hat{J}_x + i \hat{J}_y)(\hat{J}_x - i \hat{J}_y) = \hat{J}_x^2 + \hat{J}_y^2 - i[\hat{J}_x, \hat{J}_y] = \hat{J}^2 - \hat{J}_z^2 + \hbar \hat{J}_z \quad (6.14)$

$\text{同理} \quad \hat{J}_- \hat{J}_+ = \hat{J}^2 - \hat{J}_z^2 - \hbar \hat{J}_z \quad (6.15)$

综合上述，有

$[\hat{J}_z, \hat{J}_\pm] = \pm \hbar \hat{J}_\pm, \quad [\hat{J}^2, \hat{J}_\pm] = 0, \quad \hat{J}^2 = \hat{J}_\pm \hat{J}_\mp + \hat{J}_z^2 \mp \hbar \hat{J}_z \quad (6.16)$

利用这些关系，我们将可证明重要的角动量量子化定理。

> [!theorem] 定理 6-1 角动量的量子化
> 若 $\hat{J}$ 为一角动量算符，即遵守
>
> $[\hat{J}_x, \hat{J}_y] = i\hbar \hat{J}_z, \quad [\hat{J}_y, \hat{J}_z] = i\hbar \hat{J}_x, \quad [\hat{J}_z, \hat{J}_x] = i\hbar \hat{J}_y$
>
> 之对易关系，则 $\hat{J}^2$ 和 $\hat{J}_z$ 的本征值将分别为 $j(j+1)\hbar^2$ 和 $m\hbar$，且
>
> (1) $j$ 值必为正整数、半整数或零，如：0, 1/2, 1, ... 等。
>
> (2) 若 $j$ 值确定，则 $m$ 值可有 $(2j+1)$ 个可能值，并为
>
> $-j, -j+1, \dots, j-1, j$
>
> 此外，$j$ 与 $m$ 值，必同为整数或半整数。

上述定理亦可描述为：

若角动量 $\hat{J}$ 作用在状态空间中 $V$ 上，由 $\hat{J}^2$ 和 $\hat{J}_z$ 共同的本征向量 $\{|j, m\rangle\}$，必可组成空间 $V$ 的正交归一基底，并有

$\hat{J}^2 |j, m\rangle = j(j+1)\hbar^2 |j, m\rangle \quad (6.17)$

$\hat{J}_z |j, m\rangle = m\hbar |j, m\rangle \quad (6.18)$

此处 $m = -j, -j+1, \dots, j-1, j$，有 $2j+1$ 个可能值，且 $j$ 与 $m$ 必同为整数或半整数。

我们将再列出上升与下降算符的定量关系。对于此二算符 $\hat{J}_+$ 与 $\hat{J}_-$ 而言，已知

$\hat{J}_\pm |\varphi_m\rangle \propto |\varphi_{m\pm1}\rangle \quad \text{或} \quad \hat{J}_\pm |j, m\rangle = c_\pm |j, m\pm1\rangle$

$c_\pm$ 为待定之比例常数，则由 $\hat{J}_+^\dagger = \hat{J}_-$ 可得

$$
\begin{aligned} \left\|\hat{J}_\pm|j,m\rangle\right\|^2 &= \langle j,m|\hat{J}_\mp\hat{J}_\pm|j,m\rangle \\ &= \langle j,m|\hat{J}^2-\hat{J}_z^2\mp\hbar\hat{J}_z|j,m\rangle \\ &= \left[j(j+1)-m^2\mp m\right]\hbar^2=|c_\pm|^2 \\ &\Rightarrow c_\pm=\sqrt{j(j+1)-m(m\pm1)}\,\hbar \end{aligned} \quad (6.19)
$$

即

$$
\begin{aligned} \hat{J}_+ |j, m\rangle &= \sqrt{j(j+1)-m(m+1)}\,\hbar |j, m+1\rangle \\ \hat{J}_- |j, m\rangle &= \sqrt{j(j+1)-m(m-1)}\,\hbar |j, m-1\rangle \end{aligned} \quad (6.20)
$$

我们现将，由 $\hat{J}^2$ 和 $\hat{J}_z$ 共同的本征向量 $\{|j, m\rangle\}$ 做为基底（亦即可使 $\hat{J}^2$、$\hat{J}_z$ 同时表达成对角线形式）的状况下，找出 $\hat{J}_x$、$\hat{J}_y$ 的矩阵表示。

(一) $j = \frac{1}{2}$ : 在此角动量空间里, 基底

$\{\mid j,m\rangle \} = \{\mid \frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2}\rangle ,\mid \frac{1}{2}, - \frac{1}{2}\rangle \} \quad (6.21)$

或

$= \{| + \rangle ,| - \rangle \} = \{|\uparrow \rangle ,|\downarrow \rangle \} = \{\chi_{+},\chi_{-}\} \quad (6.22)$

由式(6.16)得

$\hat{J}^{2}\mid \frac{1}{2},m\rangle = \frac{1}{2}\times \frac{3}{2}\hbar^{2}\mid \frac{1}{2},m\rangle = \frac{3}{4}\hbar^{2}\mid \frac{1}{2},m\rangle \quad (6.23)$

所以

$$\hat{J}^{2} = \left( \begin{array}{cc}3\hbar^{2} / 4 & 0 \ 0 & 3\hbar^{2} / 4 \end{array} \right) = \frac{3}{4}\hbar^{2}\left( \begin{array}{cc}1 & 0 \ 0 & 1 \end{array} \right) \quad (6.24)
$$

又由式(6.17)得

$\hat{J}_{z}\mid \frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2}\rangle = \frac{1}{2}\hbar \mid \frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2}\rangle$

$\hat{J}_{z}\mid \frac{1}{2}, - \frac{1}{2}\rangle = -\frac{1}{2}\hbar \mid \frac{1}{2}, - \frac{1}{2}\rangle \quad (6.25)$

所以

$$\hat{J}_{z} = \frac{\hbar}{2}\left( \begin{array}{cc}1 & 0 \ 0 & -1 \end{array} \right) = \frac{\hbar}{2}\sigma_{z} \quad (6.26)
$$

另外, 由式(6.20)知

$\hat{J}_{\pm}\mid \frac{1}{2},m\rangle = \sqrt{\frac{1}{2} (\frac{1}{2} + 1) - m(m \pm 1)}\,\hbar \mid \frac{1}{2},m\pm 1\rangle \quad (6.27)$

得知

$$
\begin{array}{l}{\hat{J}_{+}\mid \frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2}\rangle = 0~,}\\ {\hat{J}_{-}\mid \frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2}\rangle = \hbar \mid \frac{1}{2}, - \frac{1}{2}\rangle ,\quad \hat{J}_{-}\mid \frac{1}{2}, - \frac{1}{2}\rangle = 0} \end{array} \quad (6.28)
$$

故由式(6.9)

$$
\begin{array}{l}{{\hat{J}_{x}\mid\frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2}\rangle=\frac{1}{2}(\hat{J}_{+}+\hat{J}_{-})\mid\frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2}\rangle=\frac{\hbar}{2}\mid\frac{1}{2},-\frac{1}{2}\rangle}}\\ {{\hat{J}_{x}\mid\frac{1}{2},-\frac{1}{2}\rangle=\frac{1}{2}(\hat{J}_{+}+\hat{J}_{-})\mid\frac{1}{2},-\frac{1}{2}\rangle=\frac{\hbar}{2}\mid\frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2}\rangle}}\\ {{\hat{J}_{y}\mid\frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2}\rangle=\frac{1}{2i}(\hat{J}_{+}-\hat{J}_{-})\mid\frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2}\rangle=\frac{i\hbar}{2}\mid\frac{1}{2},-\frac{1}{2}\rangle}}\\ {{\hat{J}_{y}\mid\frac{1}{2},-\frac{1}{2}\rangle=\frac{1}{2i}(\hat{J}_{+}-\hat{J}_{-})\mid\frac{1}{2},-\frac{1}{2}\rangle=-\frac{i\hbar}{2}\mid\frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2}\rangle}}\end{array} \quad (6.29)
$$

所以

$$
\begin{array}{l}{\hat{J}_{x} = \left( \begin{array}{cc}0 & \hbar /2\\ \hbar /2 & 0 \end{array} \right) = \frac{\hbar}{2}\left( \begin{array}{cc}0 & 1\\ 1 & 0 \end{array} \right) = \frac{\hbar}{2}\sigma_{x}}\\ {\hat{J}_{y} = \left( \begin{array}{cc}0 & -i\hbar /2\\ i\hbar /2 & 0 \end{array} \right) = \frac{\hbar}{2}\left( \begin{array}{cc}0 & -i\\ i & 0 \end{array} \right) = \frac{\hbar}{2}\sigma_{y}} \end{array} \quad (6.30)
$$

（二） $j = 1$ ：在此角动量空间里，基底

$\{\mid j,m\rangle \} = \{\mid 1,1\rangle ,\mid 1,0\rangle ,\mid 1, - 1\rangle \} \quad (6.32)$

由式(6.16)得

$\hat{J}^{2}\mid 1,m\rangle = 1\times 2\hbar^{2}\mid 1,m\rangle = 2\hbar^{2}\mid 1,m\rangle \quad (6.33)$

故

$$\hat{J}^{2} = \left( \begin{array}{ccc}2\hbar^{2} & 0 & 0\ 0 & 2\hbar^{2} & 0\ 0 & 0 & 2\hbar^{2} \end{array} \right) = 2\hbar^{2}\left( \begin{array}{ccc}1 & 0 & 0\ 0 & 1 & 0\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{array} \right) \quad (6.34)
$$

又由式(6.17)得

$\hat{J}_{z}\mid 1,1\rangle = \hbar \mid 1,1\rangle ;\hat{J}_{z}\mid 1,0\rangle = 0;\hat{J}_{z}\mid 1, - 1\rangle = -\hbar \mid 1, - 1\rangle \quad (6.35)$

故

$$\hat{J}_{z} = \left( \begin{array}{ccc}\hbar & 0 & 0\ 0 & 0 & 0\ 0 & 0 & -\hbar \end{array} \right) = \hbar \left( \begin{array}{ccc}1 & 0 & 0\ 0 & 0 & 0\ 0 & 0 & -1 \end{array} \right) \quad (6.36)
$$

另外，由式(6.20)知

$\hat{J}_{\pm}|1,m\rangle = \sqrt{1\times2 - m(m \pm 1)}\,\hbar |1,m \pm 1\rangle \quad (6.37)$

得

$$
\begin{array}{l l}{\hat{J}_{+}|1,1\rangle = 0~,} & {\hat{J}_{-}|1,1\rangle = \sqrt{2}\hbar |1,0\rangle ~,}\\ {\hat{J}_{+}|1,0\rangle = \sqrt{2}\hbar |1,1\rangle ~,} & {\hat{J}_{-}|1,0\rangle = \sqrt{2}\hbar |1, - 1\rangle ~,}\\ {\hat{J}_{+}|1, - 1\rangle = \sqrt{2}\hbar |1,0\rangle ,} & {\hat{J}_{-}|1, - 1\rangle = 0} \end{array} \quad (6.38)
$$

故由式(6.9)

$$
\begin{array}{l}{{\hat{J}_{x}|1,1\rangle=\frac{1}{2}(\hat{J}_{+}+\hat{J}_{-})|1,1\rangle=\frac{\hbar}{\sqrt{2}}|1,0\rangle}}\\ {{\hat{J}_{x}|1,0\rangle=\frac{\hbar}{\sqrt{2}}(|1,1\rangle+|1,-1\rangle)}}\\ {{\hat{J}_{x}|1,-1\rangle=\frac{\hbar}{\sqrt{2}}|1,0\rangle}}\\ {{\hat{J}_{y}|1,1\rangle=\frac{1}{2i}(\hat{J}_{+}-\hat{J}_{-})|1,1\rangle=\frac{i\hbar}{\sqrt{2}}|1,0\rangle}}\\ {{\hat{J}_{y}|1,0\rangle=\frac{-i\hbar}{\sqrt{2}}(|1,1\rangle-|1,-1\rangle)}}\\ {{\hat{J}_{y}|1,-1\rangle=\frac{-i\hbar}{\sqrt{2}}|1,0\rangle}}\end{array} \quad (6.39)
$$

所以

$$\hat{J}_{x} = \frac{\hbar}{\sqrt{2}}\left( \begin{array}{ccc}0 & 1 & 0\ 1 & 0 & 1\ 0 & 1 & 0 \end{array} \right), \qquad \hat{J}_{y} = \frac{\hbar}{\sqrt{2}}\left( \begin{array}{ccc}0 & -i & 0\ i & 0 & -i\ 0 & i & 0 \end{array} \right) \quad (6.40)
$$

### 问题6.7

若一系统的角动量为 $\hbar$ ，处于状态 $\frac{1}{\sqrt{14}}\left( \begin{array}{c}1\\ 2\\ 3 \end{array} \right)$ ，那么对 $\hat{L}_x$ 进行测量，得到结果为0的概率为何？得到为其他结果之概率又为何？

解 首先要找出以 $\hat{L}_z$ 为基底(或自然标准基底)所表示的 $\hat{L}_x$ 之本征向量，因

$$\hat{L}_x = \frac{\hbar}{\sqrt{2}}\left( \begin{array}{ccc}0 & 1 & 0\ 1 & 0 & 1\ 0 & 1 & 0 \end{array} \right)
$$

其特征方程

$$\det \left( \begin{array}{ccc} - \lambda & 1 & 0\ 1 & - \lambda & 1\ 0 & 1 & - \lambda \end{array} \right) = - \lambda (\lambda^2 -1) + \lambda = 2\lambda - \lambda^3 = 0\Rightarrow \lambda = 0,\pm \sqrt{2}
$$

所以 $\hat{L}_x$ 的本征值为0或 $\pm \hbar$ 。对应于0的本征向量为

$$|0\rangle_x = \left( \begin{array}{c}a\ b\ c \end{array} \right)
$$

满足 $\frac{\hbar}{\sqrt{2}}\left( \begin{array}{ccc}0 & 1 & 0\\ 1 & 0 & 1\\ 0 & 1 & 0 \end{array} \right)\left( \begin{array}{c}a\\ b\\ c \end{array} \right) = 0\Rightarrow \left\{ \begin{array}{l}b = 0\\ a + c = 0 \end{array} \right.$

归一化条件为 $a^2 + 0 + a^2 = 1\Rightarrow a = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$

所以 $|0\rangle_x = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left( \begin{array}{c}1\\ 0\\ - 1 \end{array} \right) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} (|1\rangle - | - 1\rangle)$

对应于 $\hbar$ 的本征向量 $|1\rangle_x$ 满足

$$\frac{\hbar}{\sqrt{2}}\left( \begin{array}{ccc}0 & 1 & 0\ 1 & 0 & 1\ 0 & 1 & 0 \end{array} \right)\left( \begin{array}{c}a\ b\ c \end{array} \right) = \hbar \left( \begin{array}{c}a\ b\ c \end{array} \right)\quad \text{或}\quad \left{ \begin{array}{l}b = \sqrt{2} a\ a + c = \sqrt{2} b\ b = \sqrt{2} c \end{array} \right.\Rightarrow b = \sqrt{2} a = \sqrt{2} c
$$

归一化条件为 $a^2 + (\sqrt{2} a)^2 + a^2 = 1 \Rightarrow a = \frac{1}{2}$

所以 $|1\rangle_x = \frac{1}{2} \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ \sqrt{2} \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} = \frac{1}{2} (|1\rangle + \sqrt{2} |0\rangle + |-1\rangle)$

对应于的本征向量 $|-1\rangle_x$ 满足

$$
\frac{\hbar}{\sqrt{2}} \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} a \\ b \\ c \end{pmatrix} = -\hbar \begin{pmatrix} a \\ b \\ c \end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
\Rightarrow \begin{cases} b = -\sqrt{2}a \\ a + c = -\sqrt{2}b \\ b = -\sqrt{2}c \end{cases} \Rightarrow b = -\sqrt{2}a = -\sqrt{2}c
$$

归一化条件为 $a^2 + (\sqrt{2} a)^2 + a^2 = 1 \Rightarrow a = \frac{1}{2}$

所以 $|-1\rangle_x = \frac{1}{2} \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -\sqrt{2} \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} = \frac{1}{2} (|1\rangle - \sqrt{2} |0\rangle + |-1\rangle)$

故系统所处的状态，以本征态为基底，并由完全性关系，可表为

$$
|\alpha\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{14}} \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix} = |1\rangle_{x}\,{}_{x}\langle 1 | \alpha\rangle + |0\rangle_{x}\,{}_{x}\langle 0 | \alpha\rangle + |-1\rangle_{x}\,{}_{x}\langle -1 | \alpha\rangle
$$

其中 ${}_x \langle 1 | \alpha\rangle = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{14}} (1 + 2\sqrt{2} + 3) = \frac{2 + \sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{14}}$

${}_{x} \langle 0 | \alpha\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{28}} (1 - 3) = -\frac{1}{\sqrt{7}}, \quad {}_x \langle -1 | \alpha\rangle = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{14}} (1 - 2\sqrt{2} + 3) = \frac{2 - \sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{14}}$

所以测量 $\hat{L}_x$，得到结果为 0 的概率为

$P_x(0) = |{}_x \langle 0 | \alpha\rangle|^2 = \frac{1}{7}$

得到 $\hat{L}_x$ 为其他结果 $\hbar$ 与 $-\hbar$ 之概率，则分别为

$P_x(1) = |{}_x \langle 1 | \alpha\rangle|^2 = \frac{3 + 2\sqrt{2}}{7}, \quad P_x(-1) = |{}_x \langle -1 | \alpha\rangle|^2 = \frac{3 - 2\sqrt{2}}{7}$

### 问题6.8

假设有一旋转子处于状态 $Y_1^{-1}$，若对此旋转子做 $\hat{L}_x$ 之测量，所得之观察值为何？对应之可能性是多少？

**解** 得先求 $\hat{L}_x$ 之本征值 $\alpha$ 及本征态 $|\alpha\rangle_x$ 其中

$\hat{L}_x |\alpha\rangle_x = \alpha \hbar |\alpha\rangle_x \quad (6.103)$

又

$\hat{L}_x = \frac{1}{2} (\hat{L}_+ + \hat{L}_-) \quad (6.104)$

且在角动量 $\ell=1$ 之空间中 $\hat{L}_x$ 之本征态 $|\alpha\rangle_x$ 均可以 $\hat{L}_z$ 之本征态

$Y_1^m = |1m\rangle = |m\rangle \quad m=1,0,-1$

来表示，即

$|\alpha\rangle_x = a |1\rangle + b |0\rangle + c |-1\rangle \quad (6.105)$

上式之 $a, b, c$ 为特定值，因

$$
\begin{aligned} \hat{L}_+ |-1\rangle &= \sqrt{2}\, \hbar |0\rangle, & \hat{L}_+ |0\rangle &= \sqrt{2}\, \hbar |1\rangle \\ \hat{L}_- |1\rangle &= \sqrt{2}\, \hbar |0\rangle, & \hat{L}_- |0\rangle &= \sqrt{2}\, \hbar |-1\rangle \end{aligned} \quad (6.106)
$$

故将式 (6.104) 代入 (6.103)，并利用 (6.105) 及 (6.106)、(6.103) 可写为

$\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} [b |1\rangle + (a+c) |0\rangle + b |-1\rangle] = \alpha (a |1\rangle + b |0\rangle + c |-1\rangle)$

或

$$
\begin{pmatrix} -\sqrt{2}\alpha & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & -\sqrt{2}\alpha & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & -\sqrt{2}\alpha \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} a \\ b \\ c \end{pmatrix} = 0
$$

欲有非零解，对应之行列式值需为零，得

$\alpha(\alpha^2 - 1) = 0 \Rightarrow \alpha = 0, 1, -1$

对应之本征向量，当

$\alpha = 0 \Rightarrow b = 0 \text{ 且 } a = -c \Rightarrow |0\rangle_x = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} (|1\rangle - |-1\rangle)$

${\alpha=1\Rightarrow\quad a=c=\frac{b}{\sqrt{2}}\quad\Rightarrow\quad|1\rangle_{x}=\frac{1}{2}(|1\rangle+\sqrt{2}|0\rangle+|-1\rangle)}$

${\alpha=-1\Rightarrow\quad a=c=-\frac{b}{\sqrt{2}}\quad\Rightarrow\quad|-1\rangle_{x}=\frac{1}{2}(|1\rangle-\sqrt{2}|0\rangle+|-1\rangle)}$

故对 $\hat{L}_x$ 做测量，所得之观测值为0， $\hbar$ 及 $-\hbar$ ，对应概率为

$P_0 = |{}_x \langle 0 | -1 \rangle|^2 = \left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{2}, \qquad P_1 = |{}_x \langle 1 | -1 \rangle|^2 = \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{4}$

$P_{-1} = |{}_x \langle -1 | -1 \rangle|^2 = \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{4}$

问题6.9

一转动惯量为 I 的刚体旋转子其状态为

$$\psi(t) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{14}} \begin{pmatrix} 1 \ 2 \ 3 \end{pmatrix} e^{-iEt/\hbar}, \quad E = \frac{\hbar^2}{I}
$$

(a) 对 $\hat{L}_x$ 进行测量，得到值为 $-\hbar$ 的概率为何？

(b) 经过测量得到 $\hat{L}_x = -\hbar$ 之后，此旋转子与时间有关的状态，如何以列向量来表示？

解 (a) 此旋转子的状态为

$$\psi(t) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{14}} \begin{pmatrix} 1 \ 2 \ 3 \end{pmatrix} e^{-iEt/\hbar} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{14}} (|1\rangle + 2|0\rangle + 3|-1\rangle) e^{-iEt/\hbar} \quad (\text{其中 } E = \frac{\hbar^2}{I})
$$

得到 $\hat{L}_x$ 为 $-\hbar$ 之状态为

$|-1\rangle_x = \frac{1}{2}(|1\rangle - \sqrt{2}|0\rangle + |-1\rangle)$

故对 $\hat{L}_x$ 进行测量得到 $-\hbar$ 的概率为

$|{}_x \langle -1 | \psi(t) \rangle|^2 = \frac{1}{4} \times \frac{1}{14} \times |1 - 2\sqrt{2} + 3|^2 = \frac{1}{14} |2 - \sqrt{2}|^2 = \frac{3 - 2\sqrt{2}}{7}$

(b) 经过测量得到 $\hat{L}_x$ 的本征值为 $-\hbar$ 之后，系统的状态

$\psi(t=0) = |-1\rangle_x = \frac{1}{2}(|1\rangle - \sqrt{2}|0\rangle + |-1\rangle)$

得

$$
\psi(t) = \frac{1}{2} \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -\sqrt{2} \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} e^{-iEt/\hbar}
$$

## Ch6-5 轨道角动量在位置空间上的意义

### 量子力学中的角动量

![](Physics/QuantumMechanic/姚衍/images/Ch6-5_page0_0.jpg)

### 6.1 角动量之定义及其对易关系

> [!definition] 轨道角动量（经典力学）
> 在经典力学中，运动质点相对于参考原点之轨道角动量，定义为
>
> $\vec{\ell} = \vec{r} \times \vec{p} \quad (6.1)$

其中 $\vec{r}$ 为原点至质点之位置向量，$\vec{p}$ 为质点之线动量。$\vec{\ell}$ 在直角坐标系上之三个垂直分量，则分别为

$\ell_x = y p_z - z p_y, \quad \ell_y = z p_x - x p_z, \quad \ell_z = x p_y - y p_x \quad (6.2)$

在量子力学中，由公设二，轨道角动量及其分量则应表示成算符形式，即

$\hat{L} = \hat{R} \times \hat{P}, \quad \hat{L}_x = \hat{Y} \hat{P}_z - \hat{Z} \hat{P}_y, \quad \hat{L}_y = \hat{Z} \hat{P}_x - \hat{X} \hat{P}_z, \quad \hat{L}_z = \hat{X} \hat{P}_y - \hat{Y} \hat{P}_x \quad (6.3)$

由位置—动量关系式

$[\hat{X}, \hat{P}_x] = i\hbar, \quad [\hat{Y}, \hat{P}_y] = i\hbar, \quad [\hat{Z}, \hat{P}_z] = i\hbar \quad (6.4)$

可得出角动量 $\hat{L}_x, \hat{L}_y, \hat{L}_z$ 之间的对易关系式，会有：

$$
\begin{align*} [\hat{L}_x, \hat{L}_y] &= \hat{L}_x \hat{L}_y - \hat{L}_y \hat{L}_x \\ &= (\hat{Y} \hat{P}_z - \hat{Z} \hat{P}_y) (\hat{Z} \hat{P}_x - \hat{X} \hat{P}_z) - (\hat{Z} \hat{P}_x - \hat{X} \hat{P}_z) (\hat{Y} \hat{P}_z - \hat{Z} \hat{P}_y) \\ &= \hat{P}_z \hat{Z} (\hat{Y} \hat{P}_x - \hat{X} \hat{P}_y) + \hat{Z} \hat{P}_z (\hat{P}_y \hat{X} - \hat{P}_x \hat{Y}) \\ &= (\hat{X} \hat{P}_y - \hat{Y} \hat{P}_x) (\hat{Z} \hat{P}_z - \hat{P}_z \hat{Z}) \\ &= i\hbar \hat{L}_z \end{align*} \quad (6.5)
$$

同理可得

$[\hat{L}_y,\hat{L}_z] = i\hbar \hat{L}_x,\quad [\hat{L}_z,\hat{L}_x] = i\hbar \hat{L}_y \quad (6.6)$

我们便利用此轨道角动量算符特殊之对易关系，来推广定义量子力学中的角动量。

> [!definition] 量子力学中的角动量
> 量子力学中之角动量 $\hat{J}$ （不局限为轨道角动量 $\hat{\ell}$ ），是由三个可观测量或厄米算符 $\hat{J}_x$ 、 $\hat{J}_y$ 、 $\hat{J}_z$ 所组成。且它们彼此间须满足
>
> $[\hat{J}_x,\hat{J}_y] = i\hbar \hat{J}_z,\quad [\hat{J}_y,\hat{J}_z] = i\hbar \hat{J}_x,\quad [\hat{J}_z,\hat{J}_x] = i\hbar \hat{J}_y$
>
> 或
>
> $\hat{J}\times \hat{J} = i\hbar \hat{J}$
>
> 或 $[\hat{J}_\ell ,\hat{J}_m ] = i\hbar\epsilon_{\ell mk}\hat{J}_k$ 的循环关系式（cyclic relation）。

另外，当我们再引进一厄米算符 $\hat{J}^2$

$\hat{J}^2 = \hat{J}_x^2 +\hat{J}_y^2 +\hat{J}_z^2 \quad (6.7)$

则

$$
\begin{array}{rl} & {[\hat{J}^2,\hat{J}_x] = [\hat{J}_x^2,\hat{J}_x] + [\hat{J}_y^2,\hat{J}_x] + [\hat{J}_z^2,\hat{J}_x]}\\ & {\qquad = \hat{J}_y[\hat{J}_y,\hat{J}_x] + [\hat{J}_y,\hat{J}_x]\hat{J}_y + \hat{J}_z[\hat{J}_z,\hat{J}_x] + [\hat{J}_z,\hat{J}_x]\hat{J}_z}\\ & {\qquad = \hat{J}_y(-i\hbar \hat{J}_z) + (-i\hbar \hat{J}_z)\hat{J}_y + \hat{J}_z(i\hbar \hat{J}_y) + (i\hbar \hat{J}_y)\hat{J}_z = 0} \end{array} \quad (6.8)
$$

同理

$[\hat{J}^2,\hat{J}_y] = 0,\quad [\hat{J}^2,\hat{J}_z] = 0 \quad (6.9)$

如此，若能得到愈多彼此可对易的算符关系，愈可以协助我们扩大和获得更多信息。量子力学中的角动量理论，可以完全由上述之对易关系建立起来。

由式(6.1)~(6.3)，我们无法同时去测量与获知，角动量三个分量之实验值，但由式(6.4)~(6.6)，可以同时测量得 $\hat{J}^2$ 和 $\hat{J}_x$ 、 $\hat{J}^2$ 和 $\hat{J}_y$ ，或 $\hat{J}^2$ 和 $\hat{J}_z$ 。在不失其普遍性下，一般皆选取 $\hat{J}^2$ 与 $\hat{J}_z$ 做为单一角动量之C.S.C.O.。以后将陆续可看到，如何利用 $\hat{J}^2$ 和 $\hat{J}_z$ 来进行讨论。

### 6.2 角动量的量子化及其观测值

> [!definition] 角动量的升降算符
> 现定义两个非常特殊的角动量之上升和下降(raising and lowering)算符:
>
> $\hat{J}_+ = \hat{J}_x + i \hat{J}_y, \quad \hat{J}_- = \hat{J}_x - i \hat{J}_y \quad (6.10)$

我们底下将仅使用 $\hat{J}^2$、$\hat{J}_z$ 与 $\hat{J}_+$、$\hat{J}_-$，来揭示角动量的本征值不可能连续，而必须为量子化值。此四算符彼此间，有下列常用之对易关系：

$$
\begin{aligned} [\hat{J}_z, \hat{J}_+] &= [\hat{J}_z, \hat{J}_x] + i[\hat{J}_z, \hat{J}_y] \\ &= i\hbar \hat{J}_y + i(-i\hbar \hat{J}_x) = \hbar(\hat{J}_x + i\hat{J}_y) = \hbar \hat{J}_+ \end{aligned} \quad (6.11)
$$

$\text{同理} \quad [\hat{J}_z, \hat{J}_-] = -\hbar \hat{J}_- \quad (6.12)$

$\text{又} \quad [\hat{J}^2, \hat{J}_\pm] = [\hat{J}^2, \hat{J}_x] \pm i[\hat{J}^2, \hat{J}_y] = 0 \quad (6.13)$

$\hat{J}_+ \hat{J}_- = (\hat{J}_x + i \hat{J}_y)(\hat{J}_x - i \hat{J}_y) = \hat{J}_x^2 + \hat{J}_y^2 - i[\hat{J}_x, \hat{J}_y] = \hat{J}^2 - \hat{J}_z^2 + \hbar \hat{J}_z \quad (6.14)$

$\text{同理} \quad \hat{J}_- \hat{J}_+ = \hat{J}^2 - \hat{J}_z^2 - \hbar \hat{J}_z \quad (6.15)$

综合上述，有

$[\hat{J}_z, \hat{J}_\pm] = \pm \hbar \hat{J}_\pm, \quad [\hat{J}^2, \hat{J}_\pm] = 0, \quad \hat{J}^2 = \hat{J}_\pm \hat{J}_\mp + \hat{J}_z^2 \mp \hbar \hat{J}_z \quad (6.16)$

利用这些关系，我们将可证明重要的角动量量子化定理。

> [!theorem] 定理6-1 角动量的量子化
> 若 $\hat{J}$ 为一角动量算符，即遵守
>
> $[\hat{J}_x, \hat{J}_y] = i\hbar \hat{J}_z, \quad [\hat{J}_y, \hat{J}_z] = i\hbar \hat{J}_x, \quad [\hat{J}_z, \hat{J}_x] = i\hbar \hat{J}_y$
>
> 之对易关系，则 $\hat{J}^2$ 和 $\hat{J}_z$ 的本征值将分别为 $j(j+1)\hbar^2$ 和 $m\hbar$，且
>
> (1) $j$ 值必为正整数、半整数或零，如：0, 1/2, 1, ... 等。
>
> (2) 若 $j$ 值确定，则 $m$ 值可有 $(2j+1)$ 个可能值，并为
>
> $-j, -j+1, \dots, j-1, j$
>
> 此外，$j$ 与 $m$ 值，必同为整数或半整数。

上述定理亦可描述为：

若角动量 $\hat{J}$ 作用在状态空间中 $V$ 上，由 $\hat{J}^2$ 和 $\hat{J}_z$ 共同的本征向量 $\{|j, m\rangle\}$，必可组成空间 $V$ 的正交归一基底，并有

$\hat{J}^2 |j, m\rangle = j(j+1)\hbar^2 |j, m\rangle \quad (6.17)$

$\hat{J}_z |j, m\rangle = m\hbar |j, m\rangle \quad (6.18)$

此处 $m = -j, -j+1, \dots, j-1, j$，有 $2j+1$ 个可能值，且 $j$ 与 $m$ 必同为整数或半整数。

我们将再列出上升与下降算符的定量关系。对于此二算符 $\hat{J}_+$ 与 $\hat{J}_-$ 而言，已知

$\hat{J}_\pm |\varphi_m\rangle \propto |\varphi_{m\pm1}\rangle \quad \text{或} \quad \hat{J}_\pm |j, m\rangle = c_{\pm} |j, m\pm1\rangle$

$c_{\pm}$ 为待定之比例常数，则由 $\hat{J}_+^\dagger = \hat{J}_-$ 可得

$$
\begin{aligned} \left\|\hat{J}_\pm|j,m\rangle\right\|^2 &= \langle j,m|\hat{J}_\mp\hat{J}_\pm|j,m\rangle \\ &= \langle j,m|\hat{J}^2-\hat{J}_z^2 \mp \hbar\hat{J}_z|j,m\rangle \\ &= \left[j(j+1)-m^2\mp m\right]\hbar^2=|c_\pm|^2 \\ &\Rightarrow c_\pm = \sqrt{j(j+1)-m(m\pm1)}\,\hbar \end{aligned} \quad (6.19)
$$

即

$$
\begin{aligned} \hat{J}_+ |j,m\rangle &= \sqrt{j(j+1)-m(m+1)}\,\hbar |j,m+1\rangle \\ \hat{J}_- |j,m\rangle &= \sqrt{j(j+1)-m(m-1)}\,\hbar |j,m-1\rangle \end{aligned} \quad (6.20)
$$

我们现将，由 $\hat{J}^2$ 和 $\hat{J}_z$ 共同的本征向量 $|j, m\rangle$ 做为基底（亦即可使 $\hat{J}^2$、$\hat{J}_z$ 同时表达成对角线形式）的状况下，找出 $\hat{J}_x$、$\hat{J}_y$ 的矩阵表示。

## Ch6-6 轨道角动量算符在位置空间中的形式

### 6.3 角动量量子化在轨道角动量上的应用

因轨道角动量是角动量中的一种特例，我们将应用上节角动量量子化的结果，来证明轨道角动量 $\hat{L}^2$ 之本征值 $\ell(\ell+1)\hbar^2$ 中之 $\ell$ 必为整数。其本征态 $|j,m\rangle$，在位置空间上角度部分的振幅或投影值，将可以球谐函数 $Y_\ell^m(\theta,\varphi)$ 来表示，即

$\langle \theta, \varphi \mid j, m \rangle = Y_\ell^m(\theta, \varphi) \quad (6.41)$

其中 $\ell, m$ 均为整数，且 $-\ell \le m \le \ell$。

在位置空间 $|\vec{r}\rangle = |x,y,z\rangle = |r,\theta,\varphi\rangle$ 中，位置与动量之算符 $\hat{R} \cdot \hat{P}$ 分别对应于

$\hat{R} \to R, \quad \hat{P} \to -i\hbar\nabla \quad (6.42)$

故轨道角动量算符 $\hat{L} \to \hat{R} \times \hat{P}$ 中各分量，由式(6.3)

$\hat{L}_x = -i\hbar (y \frac{\partial}{\partial z} - z \frac{\partial}{\partial y}), \quad \hat{L}_y = -i\hbar (z \frac{\partial}{\partial x} - x \frac{\partial}{\partial z}), \quad \hat{L}_z = -i\hbar (x \frac{\partial}{\partial y} - y \frac{\partial}{\partial x}) \quad (6.43)$

兹以球面坐标 $|r,\theta,\varphi\rangle$，而非直角坐标 $|x,y,z\rangle$，来表示轨道角动量 $\hat{L}_x, \hat{L}_y, \hat{L}_z$，因

$x = r\sin\theta\cos\varphi, \quad y = r\sin\theta\sin\varphi, \quad z = r\cos\theta \quad (6.44)$

其中 $r>0, 0 \le \theta \le \pi, 0 \le \varphi \le 2\pi$；这是由球面坐标 $r,\theta,\varphi$ 表示直角坐标 $x,y,z$ 的关系式。又微小体积单元

$d^3r = r^2 \sin\theta \, dr \, d\theta \, d\varphi = r^2 dr \, d\Omega \quad (6.45)$

其中立体角 $d\Omega = \sin\theta \, d\theta \, d\varphi$。若以 $x,y,z$ 表 $r,\theta,\varphi$，则有

$r^2 = x^2 + y^2 + z^2, \quad \cos\theta = \frac{z}{r}, \quad \tan\varphi = \frac{y}{x} \quad (6.46)$

$\frac{\partial r}{\partial x} = \frac{1}{2} \left( x^2 + y^2 + z^2 \right)^{-1/2} \cdot 2x = \frac{x}{r} \quad (6.47)$

同理 $\frac{\partial r}{\partial y} = \frac{y}{r}, \frac{\partial r}{\partial z} = \frac{z}{r}$ (6.48)

$$
\begin{array}{r l} & { - \sin \theta \frac{\partial\theta}{\partial x} = \frac{\partial\theta}{\partial x}\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta}\cos \theta = \frac{\partial}{\partial x}\cos \theta = z\frac{\partial}{\partial x} (\frac{1}{r})}\\ & {\qquad = -\frac{z}{r^{2}}\frac{\partial r}{\partial x} = -\frac{z x}{r^{3}} = -\frac{1}{r}\cdot \frac{z}{r}\cdot \frac{x}{r} = -\frac{1}{r}\cos \theta \sin \theta \cos \phi} \end{array} \quad (6.48)
$$

所以 $\frac{\partial\theta}{\partial x} = \frac{\cos\theta\cos\phi}{r}$ (6.50)

同理 $-\sin \theta \frac{\partial\theta}{\partial y} = \frac{1}{r}\cos \theta \sin \theta \sin \phi , - \sin \theta \frac{\partial\theta}{\partial z} = \frac{1}{r} (1 - \cos^2\theta)$ (6.51)

故 $\frac{\partial\theta}{\partial y} = \frac{\cos\theta\sin\phi}{r}, \frac{\partial\theta}{\partial z} = -\frac{\sin\theta}{r}$ (6.52)

而 $\sec^2 \phi \frac{\partial\phi}{\partial x} = \frac{\partial}{\partial x}\tan \phi = y\frac{\partial}{\partial x} (\frac{1}{x}) = -\frac{y}{x^2}$ (6.53)

因此 $\frac{\partial\phi}{\partial x} = -\frac{y}{x^2}\cos^2\phi$ (6.54)

同理 $\frac{\partial\phi}{\partial y} = -\frac{\cos^2\phi}{x}, \frac{\partial\phi}{\partial z} = 0$ (6.55)

利用以上的结果，可将对直角坐标 $x,y,z$ 之偏导数，全部转成对球面坐标 $r,\theta ,\phi$ 之偏导数：

$\frac{\partial}{\partial x} = \frac{\partial r}{\partial x}\frac{\partial}{\partial r} +\frac{\partial\theta}{\partial x}\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} +\frac{\partial\phi}{\partial x}\frac{\partial}{\partial\phi} = \frac{x}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial r} +\frac{\cos\theta\cos\phi}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} -\frac{y}{x^2}\cos^2\phi \frac{\partial}{\partial\phi} \quad (6.56)$

$\frac{\partial}{\partial y} = \frac{\partial r}{\partial y}\frac{\partial}{\partial r} +\frac{\partial\theta}{\partial y}\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} +\frac{\partial\phi}{\partial y}\frac{\partial}{\partial\phi} = \frac{y}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial r} +\frac{\cos\theta\sin\phi}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} +\frac{\cos^2\phi}{x}\frac{\partial}{\partial\phi} \quad (6.57)$

$\frac{\partial}{\partial z} = \frac{\partial r}{\partial z}\frac{\partial}{\partial r} +\frac{\partial\theta}{\partial z}\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} +\frac{\partial\phi}{\partial z}\frac{\partial}{\partial\phi} = \frac{z}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial r} -\frac{\sin\theta}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} \quad (6.58)$

再利用式(6.43)，遂可用 $r,\theta ,\phi$ 表示出角动量在直角坐标分量之算符形式

$$
\begin{array}{rl} & {\hat{L}_x = -i\hbar (y\frac{\partial}{\partial z} -z\frac{\partial}{\partial y})}\\ & {\qquad = -i\hbar (\frac{yz}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial r} -y\frac{\sin\theta}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial \theta} -y\frac{z}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial r} -\frac{z\cos \theta \sin \phi}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial \theta} -z\cos^2\phi \frac{\partial}{\partial \phi})}\\ & {\qquad = i\hbar (\sin \phi \frac{\partial}{\partial \theta} +\frac{\cos \phi}{\tan \theta}\frac{\partial}{\partial \phi})} \end{array} \quad (6.59)
$$

$$
\begin{array}{rl} & {\hat{L}_y = -i\hbar (z\frac{\partial}{\partial x} -x\frac{\partial}{\partial z})}\\ & {\qquad = -i\hbar (\frac{xz}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial r} -z\cos \theta \cos \phi \frac{\partial}{\partial \theta} -y\frac{yz}{x^2}\cos^2\phi \frac{\partial}{\partial \phi} -\frac{xz}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial r} +x\sin \theta \frac{\partial}{\partial \theta})}\\ & {\qquad = i\hbar (-\cos \phi \frac{\partial}{\partial \theta} +\frac{\sin \phi}{\tan \theta}\frac{\partial}{\partial \phi})} \end{array} \quad (6.60)
$$

$$
\begin{array}{rl} & {\hat{L}_z = -i\hbar (x\frac{\partial}{\partial y} -y\frac{\partial}{\partial x})}\\ & {\qquad = -i\hbar (\frac{xy}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial r} +\frac{x\cos\theta\sin\phi}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta})}\\ & {\qquad \qquad \qquad +\frac{x\cos^2\phi}{x}\frac{\partial}{\partial\phi} -\frac{xy}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial r} -y\cos \theta \cos \phi \frac{\partial}{\partial \theta} +\frac{y^2}{x^2}\cos^2\phi \frac{\partial}{\partial \phi})}\\ & {\qquad = -i\hbar \frac{\partial}{\partial\phi}} \end{array} \quad (6.61)
$$

因此得到

> [!definition] 轨道角动量在球坐标中的算符表示
>
> $$
> \begin{array}{rl} & {\hat{L}^2 = \hat{L}_x^2 +\hat{L}_y^2 +\hat{L}_z^2 = -\hbar^2 (\frac{\partial^2}{\partial\theta^2} +\frac{1}{\tan\theta}\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} +\frac{1}{\sin^2\theta}\frac{\partial^2}{\partial\phi^2})}\\ & {\qquad \hat{L}_+ = \hat{L}_x + i\hat{L}_y = \hbar e^{i\phi} (\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} +i\cot \theta \frac{\partial}{\partial\phi})}\\ & {\qquad \hat{L}_- = \hat{L}_x - i\hat{L}_y = \hbar e^{-i\phi} (-\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} +i\cot \theta \frac{\partial}{\partial\phi})} \end{array} \quad (6.62)
> $$

已知 $\hat{L}$ 满足角动量的定义(6.4)~(6.5), 故由角动量量子化定理 6.1, 所作用的状态空间里, $\hat{L}^2$ 和 $\hat{L}_z$ 享有共同之本征态 $|\ell , m\rangle$ , 并可给出对应之本征值 $\ell (\ell + 1)\hbar^2$ 与 $m\hbar$ 。

$$
\begin{array}{l}\frac{\partial}{\partial x} = \frac{\partial r}{\partial x}\frac{\partial}{\partial r} +\frac{\partial \theta}{\partial x}\frac{\partial}{\partial \theta} +\frac{\partial \phi}{\partial x}\frac{\partial}{\partial \phi} = \frac{x}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial r} +\frac{\cos \theta \cos \phi}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial \theta} -\frac{y}{x^2}\cos^2\phi \frac{\partial}{\partial \phi}\\ \displaystyle \frac{\partial}{\partial y} = \frac{\partial r}{\partial y}\frac{\partial}{\partial r} +\frac{\partial \theta}{\partial y}\frac{\partial}{\partial \theta} +\frac{\partial \phi}{\partial y}\frac{\partial}{\partial \phi} = \frac{y}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial r} +\frac{\cos \theta \sin \phi}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial \theta} +\frac{\cos^2\phi}{x}\frac{\partial}{\partial \phi}\\ \displaystyle \frac{\partial}{\partial z} = \frac{\partial r}{\partial z}\frac{\partial}{\partial r} +\frac{\partial \theta}{\partial z}\frac{\partial}{\partial \theta} +\frac{\partial \phi}{\partial z}\frac{\partial}{\partial \phi} = \frac{z}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial r} -\frac{\sin\theta}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial \theta} \end{array} \quad (6.58)
$$

再利用式(6.43)，遂可用 $r, \theta , \phi$ 表示出角动量在直角坐标分量之算符形式

$$
\begin{array}{rl} & {\hat{L}_x = -i\hbar (y\frac{\partial}{\partial z} -z\frac{\partial}{\partial y})}\\ & {\qquad = -i\hbar (\frac{yz}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial r} -\frac{y\sin\theta}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} -\frac{yz}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial r} +\frac{z\cos\theta\sin\phi}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} -\frac{z\cos^2\phi}{x}\frac{\partial}{\partial\phi})}\\ & {\qquad = i\hbar (\sin \phi \frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} +\frac{\cos\phi}{\tan\theta}\frac{\partial}{\partial\phi})} \end{array} \quad (6.59)
$$

$$
\begin{array}{rl} & {\hat{L}_y = -i\hbar (z\frac{\partial}{\partial x} -x\frac{\partial}{\partial z})}\\ & {\qquad = -i\hbar (\frac{xz}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial r} -\frac{z\cos\theta\cos\phi}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} -\frac{yz}{x^2}\cos^2\phi \frac{\partial}{\partial\phi} -\frac{xz}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial r} +\frac{x\sin\theta}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta})}\\ & {\qquad = i\hbar (-\cos \phi \frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} +\frac{\sin\phi}{\tan\theta}\frac{\partial}{\partial\phi})} \end{array} \quad (6.60)
$$

$$
\begin{array}{rl} & {\hat{L}_z = -i\hbar (x\frac{\partial}{\partial y} -y\frac{\partial}{\partial x})}\\ & {\qquad = -i\hbar (\frac{xy}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial r} +\frac{x\cos\theta\sin\phi}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta})}\\ & {\qquad \qquad +\frac{x\cos^2\phi}{x}\frac{\partial}{\partial\phi} -\frac{xy}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial r} -\frac{y\cos\theta\cos\phi}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} +\frac{y^2}{x^2}\cos^2\phi \frac{\partial}{\partial\phi})}\\ & {\qquad = -i\hbar \frac{\partial}{\partial\phi}} \end{array} \quad (6.61)
$$

因此得到

$$
\begin{array}{rl} & {\hat{L}^2 = \hat{L}_x^2 +\hat{L}_y^2 +\hat{L}_z^2 = -\hbar^2 (\frac{\partial^2}{\partial\theta^2} +\frac{1}{\tan\theta}\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} +\frac{1}{\sin^2\theta}\frac{\partial^2}{\partial\phi^2})}\\ & {\qquad \hat{L}_+ = \hat{L}_x + i\hat{L}_y = \hbar e^{i\phi} (\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} +i\cot \theta \frac{\partial}{\partial\phi})}\\ & {\qquad \hat{L}_- = \hat{L}_x - i\hat{L}_y = \hbar e^{-i\phi} (-\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} +i\cot \theta \frac{\partial}{\partial\phi})} \end{array} \quad (6.63)
$$

已知 $\hat{L}$ 满足角动量的定义(6.4)~(6.5)，故由角动量量子化定理6.1，所作用的状态空间里， $\hat{L}^2$ 和 $\hat{L}_z$ 享有共同之本征态 $|\ell ,m\rangle$ ，并可给出对应之本征值 $\ell (\ell +1)\hbar^2$ 与 $m\hbar$ 。

  & \quad - i \hbar \frac{\partial}{\partial \varphi} \Psi\_{\ell m}(r, \theta, \varphi) = m \hbar \Psi\_{\ell m}(r, \theta, \varphi) \tag{6.68}
  \end{align*}

$$

式中 $\Psi_{\ell m}(r, \theta, \varphi)$ 代表状态 $|\ell, m\rangle$ 在 $|\vec{r}\rangle = |r, \theta, \varphi\rangle$ 上之投影值。令

\begin{align*}

- \hbar^2 \left( \frac{\partial^2}{\partial \theta^2} + \frac{1}{\tan \theta} \frac{\partial}{\partial \theta} + \frac{1}{\sin^2 \theta} \frac{\partial^2}{\partial \varphi^2} \right) Y\_{\ell}^{m}(\theta, \varphi) &= \ell(\ell+1) \hbar^2 Y\_{\ell}^{m}(\theta, \varphi) \tag{6.69} \\

## Ch6-8 轨道角动量算符的本征函数

$$

\begin{align*}
\hat{L}\_x &= -i\hbar \left( y \frac{\partial}{\partial z} - z \frac{\partial}{\partial y} \right) \\
&= -i\hbar \left( \frac{yz}{r} \frac{\partial}{\partial r} - \frac{y\sin\theta}{r} \frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} - \frac{yz}{r} \frac{\partial}{\partial r} - \frac{z\cos\theta\sin\varphi}{r} \frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} - \frac{z\cos^2\varphi}{x} \frac{\partial}{\partial\varphi} \right) \\

\end{align*}
\qquad (6.59)

$$

\begin{align\*}
\hat{L}\_y &= -i\hbar \left( z \frac{\partial}{\partial x} - x \frac{\partial}{\partial z} \right) \\

\end{align\*}

\begin{align*}
\hat{L}\_z &= -i\hbar \left( x \frac{\partial}{\partial y} - y \frac{\partial}{\partial x} \right) \\

\end{align*}
\qquad (6.61)

$$

因此得到

\hat{L}\_+ = \hat{L}\_x + i\hat{L}\_y = \hbar e^{i\varphi} \left( \frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} + i\cot\theta \frac{\partial}{\partial\varphi} \right) \quad (6.63)

\hat{L}\_- = \hat{L}\_x - i\hat{L}\_y = \hbar e^{-i\varphi} \left( -\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} + i\cot\theta \frac{\partial}{\partial\varphi} \right) \quad (6.64)

$$

已知 $\hat{L}$ 满足角动量的定义(6.4)~(6.5)，故由角动量量子化定理 6.1，所作
用的状态空间里，$\hat{L}^2$ 和 $\hat{L}_z$ 享有共同之本征态 $|\ell, m\rangle$，并可给出对应

>
> $$
> \begin{array}{l} \hat{L}^2 |\ell,m\rangle = \ell(\ell+1)\hbar^2 |\ell,m\rangle \\ \hat{L}_z |\ell,m\rangle = m\hbar |\ell,m\rangle \end{array} \quad (6.65) \quad (6.66)
> $$

将上式投影在位置空间 $|\vec{r}\rangle = |r,\theta,\varphi\rangle$ 后，由式(6.65)与(6.66)得到

$$
\begin{aligned} -\hbar^2 \left( \frac{\partial^2}{\partial\theta^2} + \frac{1}{\tan\theta} \frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} + \frac{1}{\sin^2\theta} \frac{\partial^2}{\partial\varphi^2} \right) \Psi_{\ell m}(r,\theta,\varphi) &= \ell(\ell+1)\hbar^2 \Psi_{\ell m}(r,\theta,\varphi) \quad (6.67) \\ &-i\hbar \frac{\partial}{\partial\varphi} \Psi_{\ell m}(r,\theta,\varphi) = m\hbar \Psi_{\ell m}(r,\theta,\varphi) \end{aligned} \quad (6.68)
$$

式中 $\Psi_{\ell m}(r,\theta,\varphi)$ 代表状态 $|\ell,m\rangle$ 在 $|\vec{r}\rangle = |r,\theta,\varphi\rangle$ 上之投影值。令
$\Psi_{\ell m}(r,\theta,\varphi) = f(r) Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta,\varphi)$，则

$$
\begin{aligned} -\hbar^2 \left( \frac{\partial^2}{\partial\theta^2} + \frac{1}{\tan\theta} \frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} + \frac{1}{\sin^2\theta} \frac{\partial^2}{\partial\varphi^2} \right) Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta,\varphi) &= \ell(\ell+1)\hbar^2 Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta,\varphi) \quad (6.69) \\ &-i\hbar \frac{\partial}{\partial\varphi} Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta,\varphi) = m\hbar Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta,\varphi) \end{aligned} \quad (6.70)
$$

再令 $Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta,\varphi) = \Theta(\theta)\Phi(\theta)$，得

$-i\hbar \frac{\partial}{\partial\varphi} \Phi(\varphi) = m\hbar \Phi(\varphi) \quad (6.71)$

故 $\Phi(\varphi) = e^{im\varphi}$ 或 $Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta,\varphi) = \Theta_{\ell m}(\theta)e^{im\varphi}$

因一函数同一点，不能对应到不同函数值。故可形成 $\varphi$ 之边界条件：

$Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta,\varphi=0) = Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta,\varphi=2\pi) \quad (6.73)$

也就是说，$e^0 = e^{im\cdot 2\pi}$，或

$$
1 = \cos 2m\pi + i\sin 2m\pi \Rightarrow \begin{cases} m = \text{整数} = 0, \pm 1, \pm 2, \dots \\ m = \text{整数} = 0, \pm 1, \pm 2, \dots \end{cases} \quad (6.74)
$$

但由定理 6.1 $m = -\ell, -\ell+1, -\ell+2, \dots, \ell-1, \ell$，所以必然地

$\ell = \text{整数} \quad (6.75)$

亦即 $\hat{L}^2$ 和 $\hat{L}_z$ 的本征值均为整数。现再以 $Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta , \phi) = \Theta (\theta) \Phi (\phi)$ 代入可得

$(\frac{d^2}{d\theta^2} +\frac{1}{\tan\theta}\frac{d}{d\theta} +\frac{(im)^2}{\sin^2\theta})\Theta (\theta) = -\ell (\ell +1)\Theta (\theta) \quad (6.76)$

或 $\frac{1}{\sin\theta}\frac{d}{d\theta} (\sin \theta \frac{d\Theta(\theta)}{d\theta}) + [\ell (\ell + 1) - \frac{m^2}{\sin^2\theta} ]\Theta (\theta) = 0$

再令新变量 $x$ （非直角坐标分量 $x$ ）

$x = \cos \theta , \frac{d}{d\theta} = \frac{dx}{d\theta}\frac{d}{dx} = -\sin \theta \frac{d}{dx} \quad (6.78)$

则 $\frac{d}{dx} [(1 - x^2)\frac{d\Theta(x)}{dx} ] + [\ell (\ell + 1) - \frac{m^2}{1 - x^2} ]\Theta (x) = 0$

$\frac{d^2}{dx^2}\Theta (x) - 2x\frac{d}{dx}\Theta (x) + [\ell (\ell + 1) - \frac{m^2}{1 - x^2} ]\Theta (x) = 0 \quad (6.80)$

这就是连带的勒让德（Legendre）微分方程的形式，其解已知为（参考附录）：

$\Theta_{\ell m}(x) = P_{\ell}^{m}(x) = (1 - x^2)^{m / 2}\frac{d^m}{dx^m} P_{\ell}(x) \quad (6.81)$

式中

$P_{\ell}(x) = \frac{1}{2^{\ell}\ell!}\frac{d^{\ell}}{dx^{\ell}}(x^{2} - 1)^{\ell} \quad (6.82)$

而 $P_{\ell}(x)$ 和 $P_{\ell}^{m}(x)$ ，分别称为 Legendre，和连带的 Legendre 多项式。因此

$Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta , \phi) = c\Theta_{\ell m}(\theta)e^{im\phi} = cP_{\ell}^{m}(\theta)e^{im\phi} \quad (6.83)$

$c$ 为待定之归一化常数。由归一化条件

$1 = \int_{0}^{2\pi}\int_{0}^{\pi}Y_{\ell}^{m*}(\theta,\phi)Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta,\phi)\sin\theta \, d\theta \, d\phi = 2\pi|c|^{2}\int_{0}^{\pi}P_{\ell}^{m*}(\theta)P_{\ell}^{m}(\theta)\sin\theta \, d\theta = 2\pi|c|^{2}\int_{-1}^{1}P_{\ell}^{m}(x)P_{\ell}^{m}(x)\, dx = |c|^{2}\frac{4\pi}{2\ell+1}\frac{(\ell+m)!}{(\ell-m)!}$

所以

$Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta, \varphi) = \sqrt{\frac{2\ell+1}{4\pi} \frac{(\ell-m)!}{(\ell+m)!}} P_{\ell}^{m}(\theta) e^{im\varphi} \quad (6.86)$

> [!definition] 球谐函数
> $Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta, \varphi) = \sqrt{\frac{2\ell+1}{4\pi} \frac{(\ell-m)!}{(\ell+m)!}} P_{\ell}^{m}(\theta) e^{im\varphi}$
>
> 称为球谐函数 (spherical harmonics)。它是 $\hat{L}^2$ 和 $\hat{L}_z$ 之本征态 $|\ell, m\rangle$ 在位置坐标 $(\theta, \varphi)$ 上之投影值，即
> $Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta, \varphi) = \langle \theta, \varphi | \ell, m \rangle \quad (6.87)$

一些常用的球谐函数 $Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta, \varphi)$ 为（参考附录）：

$$
\begin{align*} Y_0^0 &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{4\pi}}, \quad Y_1^0 = \sqrt{\frac{3}{4\pi}} \cos\theta = \sqrt{\frac{3}{4\pi}} \frac{z}{r}, \quad Y_1^{\pm 1} = \mp \sqrt{\frac{3}{8\pi}} e^{\pm i\varphi} \sin\theta = \mp \sqrt{\frac{3}{8\pi}} \frac{x \pm iy}{r} \\ Y_2^0 &= \sqrt{\frac{5}{16\pi}} (3\cos^2\theta - 1) = \sqrt{\frac{5}{16\pi}} \frac{2z^2 - x^2 - y^2}{r^2} \\ Y_2^{\pm 1} &= \mp \sqrt{\frac{15}{8\pi}} e^{\pm i\varphi} \cos\theta \sin\theta = \mp \sqrt{\frac{15}{8\pi}} \frac{(x \pm iy)z}{r^2} \\ Y_2^{\pm 2} &= \sqrt{\frac{15}{32\pi}} e^{\pm 2i\varphi} \sin^2\theta = \sqrt{\frac{15}{32\pi}} \frac{(x \pm iy)^2}{r^2} \end{align*} \quad (6.88)
$$

对轨道角动量的结果，现可结论于下：

再令 $Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta, \varphi) = \Theta(\theta)\Phi(\varphi)$，得

$$

-i\hbar \frac{\partial}{\partial \varphi} \Phi(\varphi) = m\hbar \Phi(\varphi) \tag{6.71}

$$

故 $\Phi(\varphi) = e^{im\varphi}$ 或 $Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta, \varphi) = \Theta_{\ell m}(\theta)e^{im\varphi}$ (6.72)

因一函数在同一点，不能对应到不同函数值。故可形成 $\varphi$ 之边界条件：

$$

Y\_{\ell}^{m}(\theta, \varphi = 0) = Y\_{\ell}^{m}(\theta, \varphi = 2\pi) \quad (6.73)

$$

也就是说，$e^0 = e^{im \cdot 2\pi}$，或

$$

1 = \cos 2m\pi + i\sin 2m\pi \Rightarrow m = \text{整数} = 0, \pm 1, \pm 2, \dots \quad (6.74)

$$

但由定理 6.1 $m = -\ell, -\ell+1, -\ell+2, \dots, \ell-1, \ell$，所以必然地

$$

\ell = \text{整数} \quad (6.75)

$$

所以

> [!definition] 球谐函数（spherical harmonics）
> $Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta, \varphi) = \sqrt{\frac{2\ell+1}{4\pi} \frac{(\ell-m)!}{(\ell+m)!}} P_{\ell}^{m}(\theta) e^{im\varphi} \quad (6.86)$
>
> 称为球谐函数(spherical harmonics)。它是 $\hat{L}^2$ 和 $\hat{L}_z$ 之本征态
> $|\ell, m\rangle$ 在位置坐标 $(\theta, \varphi)$ 上之投影值，即
>
> $Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta, \varphi) = \langle \theta, \varphi | \ell, m \rangle \quad (6.87)$

一些常用的球谐函数 $Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta, \varphi)$ 为（参考附录）：

$Y_0^0 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{4\pi}}, \quad Y_1^0 = \sqrt{\frac{3}{4\pi}} \cos \theta = \sqrt{\frac{3}{4\pi}} \frac{z}{r}, \quad Y_1^{\pm 1} = \mp \sqrt{\frac{3}{8\pi}} e^{\pm i\varphi} \sin \theta = \mp \sqrt{\frac{3}{8\pi}} \frac{x \pm iy}{r}$

$Y_2^0 = \sqrt{\frac{5}{16\pi}} (3\cos^2\theta - 1) = \sqrt{\frac{5}{16\pi}} \frac{2z^2 - x^2 - y^2}{r^2}$

$Y_2^{\pm 1} = \mp \sqrt{\frac{15}{8\pi}} e^{\pm i\varphi} \cos \theta \sin \theta = \mp \sqrt{\frac{15}{8\pi}} \frac{(x \pm iy)z}{r^2}$

$Y_2^{\pm 2} = \sqrt{\frac{15}{32\pi}} e^{\pm 2i\varphi} \sin^2\theta = \sqrt{\frac{15}{32\pi}} \frac{(x \pm iy)^2}{r^2} \quad (6.88)$

对轨道角动量的结果，现可结论于下：

> [!theorem] 轨道角动量本征值方程之解
> 满足轨道角动量算符 $\hat{L}^2$ 与 $\hat{L}_z$ 之本征值方程
>
> $\hat{L}^2 \Psi_{\ell m} = \ell (\ell + 1) \hbar^2 \Psi_{\ell m} \quad \text{与} \quad \hat{L}_z \Psi_{\ell m} = m \hbar \Psi_{\ell m}$
>
> 其本征函数必为球谐函数
>
> $\Psi_{\ell m}(\theta, \varphi) = Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta, \varphi)$
>
> 且本征值 $\ell \cdot m$ 皆为整数。若 $\ell$ 已给定，则
>
> $m = -\ell, -\ell+1, \dots 0, 1, 2, \dots \ell-1, \ell$

## Ch6-10 自旋与空间量子化

$$

\begin{align*}
\hat{L}\_x &= -i\hbar \left( y \frac{\partial}{\partial z} - z \frac{\partial}{\partial y} \right) \\
&= -i\hbar \left( \frac{yz}{r} \frac{\partial}{\partial r} - \frac{y\sin\theta}{r} \frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} - \frac{yz}{r} \frac{\partial}{\partial r} - \frac{z\cos\theta\sin\varphi}{r} \frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} - \frac{z\cos^2\varphi}{x} \frac{\partial}{\partial\varphi} \right) \\
&= i\hbar \left( \sin\varphi \frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} + \frac{\cos\varphi}{\tan\theta} \frac{\partial}{\partial\varphi} \right)
\end{align*}
\qquad (6.59)

$$

\begin{align\*}
\hat{L}\_y &= -i\hbar \left( z \frac{\partial}{\partial x} - x \frac{\partial}{\partial z} \right) \\
&= -i\hbar \left( \frac{xz}{r} \frac{\partial}{\partial r} - \frac{z\cos\theta\cos\varphi}{r} \frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} - \frac{yz}{x^2} \cos^2\varphi \frac{\partial}{\partial\varphi} - \frac{xz}{r} \frac{\partial}{\partial r} + \frac{x\sin\theta}{r} \frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} \right) \\
&= i\hbar \left( -\cos\varphi \frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} + \frac{\sin\varphi}{\tan\theta} \frac{\partial}{\partial\varphi} \right)
\end{align\*}
\qquad (6.60)

$$

\begin{align*}
\hat{L}\_z &= -i\hbar \left( x \frac{\partial}{\partial y} - y \frac{\partial}{\partial x} \right) \\
&= -i\hbar \left( \frac{xy}{r} \frac{\partial}{\partial r} + \frac{x\cos\theta\sin\varphi}{r} \frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} + \frac{x\cos^2\varphi}{x} \frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} - \frac{y\cos\theta\cos\varphi}{r} \frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} + \frac{y^2}{x^2} \cos^2\varphi \frac{\partial}{\partial\varphi} \right) \\
&= -i\hbar \frac{\partial}{\partial\varphi}
\end{align*}
\qquad (6.61)

$$

因此得到

$$

\hat{L}^2 = \hat{L}\_x^2 + \hat{L}\_y^2 + \hat{L}\_z^2 = -\hbar^2 \left( \frac{\partial^2}{\partial\theta^2} + \frac{1}{\tan\theta} \frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} + \frac{1}{\sin^2\theta} \frac{\partial^2}{\partial\varphi^2} \right) \quad (6.62)

$$

\hat{L}\_+ = \hat{L}\_x + i\hat{L}\_y = \hbar e^{i\varphi} \left( \frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} + i\cot\theta \frac{\partial}{\partial\varphi} \right) \quad (6.63)

$$

\hat{L}\_- = \hat{L}\_x - i\hat{L}\_y = \hbar e^{-i\varphi} \left( -\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} + i\cot\theta \frac{\partial}{\partial\varphi} \right) \quad (6.64)

$$

已知 $\hat{L}$ 满足角动量的定义(6.4)~(6.5)，故由角动量量子化定理 6.1，所作
用的状态空间里，$\hat{L}^2$ 和 $\hat{L}_z$ 享有共同之本征态 $|\ell, m\rangle$，并可给出对应
之本征值 $\ell (\ell + 1)\hbar^2$ 与 $m\hbar$。

> [!equation] 轨道角动量本征值方程
>
> $$
> \begin{array}{l} \hat{L}^2 |\ell,m\rangle = \ell(\ell+1)\hbar^2 |\ell,m\rangle \\ \hat{L}_z |\ell,m\rangle = m\hbar |\ell,m\rangle \end{array} \quad (6.65) \quad (6.66)
> $$

将上式投影在位置空间 $|\vec{r}\rangle = |r,\theta,\varphi\rangle$ 后，由式(6.65)与(6.66)得到

$$
\begin{aligned} -\hbar^2 \left( \frac{\partial^2}{\partial\theta^2} + \frac{1}{\tan\theta} \frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} + \frac{1}{\sin^2\theta} \frac{\partial^2}{\partial\varphi^2} \right) \Psi_{\ell m}(r,\theta,\varphi) &= \ell(\ell+1)\hbar^2 \Psi_{\ell m}(r,\theta,\varphi) \quad (6.67) \\ &-i\hbar \frac{\partial}{\partial\varphi} \Psi_{\ell m}(r,\theta,\varphi) = m\hbar \Psi_{\ell m}(r,\theta,\varphi) \end{aligned} \quad (6.68)
$$

式中 $\Psi_{\ell m}(r,\theta,\varphi)$ 代表状态 $|\ell,m\rangle$ 在 $|\vec{r}\rangle = |r,\theta,\varphi\rangle$ 上之投影值。令
$\Psi_{\ell m}(r,\theta,\varphi) = f(r) Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta,\varphi)$，则

$$
\begin{aligned} -\hbar^2 \left( \frac{\partial^2}{\partial\theta^2} + \frac{1}{\tan\theta} \frac{\partial}{\partial\theta} + \frac{1}{\sin^2\theta} \frac{\partial^2}{\partial\varphi^2} \right) Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta,\varphi) &= \ell(\ell+1)\hbar^2 Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta,\varphi) \quad (6.69) \\ &-i\hbar \frac{\partial}{\partial\varphi} Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta,\varphi) = m\hbar Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta,\varphi) \end{aligned} \quad (6.70)
$$

再令 $Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta,\varphi) = \Theta(\theta)\Phi(\theta)$，得

$-i\hbar \frac{\partial}{\partial\varphi} \Phi(\varphi) = m\hbar \Phi(\varphi) \quad (6.71)$

故 $\Phi(\varphi) = e^{im\varphi}$ 或 $Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta,\varphi) = \Theta_{\ell m}(\theta)e^{im\varphi}$ (6.72)

因一函数同一点，不能对应到不同函数值。故可形成 $\varphi$ 之边界条件：

$Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta, \varphi = 0) = Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta, \varphi = 2\pi) \quad (6.73)$

也就是说，$e^0 = e^{im\cdot 2\pi}$，或

$$
1 = \cos 2m\pi + i\sin 2m\pi \Rightarrow \begin{cases} m = \text{整数} = 0, \pm 1, \pm 2, \dots \\ m = \text{整数} = 0, \pm 1, \pm 2, \dots \end{cases} \quad (6.74)
$$

但由定理 6.1 $m = -\ell, -\ell+1, -\ell+2, \dots, \ell-1, \ell$，所以必然地

$\ell = \text{整数} \quad (6.75)$

亦即 $\hat{L}^2$ 和 $\hat{L}_z$ 的本征值均为整数。现再以 $Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta , \phi) = \Theta (\theta) \Phi (\phi)$ 代入可得

$(\frac{d^2}{d\theta^2} +\frac{1}{\tan\theta}\frac{d}{d\theta} +\frac{(im)^2}{\sin^2\theta})\Theta (\theta) = -\ell (\ell +1)\Theta (\theta) \quad (6.76)$

或 $\frac{1}{\sin\theta}\frac{d}{d\theta} (\sin \theta \frac{d\Theta(\theta)}{d\theta}) + [\ell (\ell + 1) - \frac{m^2}{\sin^2\theta} ]\Theta (\theta) = 0$

再令新变量 $x$ （非直角坐标分量 $x$ ）

$x = \cos \theta , \frac{d}{d\theta} = \frac{dx}{d\theta}\frac{d}{dx} = -\sin \theta \frac{d}{dx} \quad (6.78)$

则 $\frac{d}{dx} [(1 - x^2)\frac{d\Theta(x)}{dx} ] + [\ell (\ell + 1) - \frac{m^2}{1 - x^2} ]\Theta (x) = 0$

$\frac{d^2}{dx^2}\Theta (x) - 2x\frac{d}{dx}\Theta (x) + [\ell (\ell + 1) - \frac{m^2}{1 - x^2} ]\Theta (x) = 0 \quad (6.80)$

这就是连带的勒让德（Legendre）微分方程的形式，其解已知为（参考附录）：

$\Theta_{\ell m}(x) = P_{\ell}^{m}(x) = (1 - x^2)^{m / 2}\frac{d^m}{dx^m} P_{\ell}(x) \quad (6.81)$

式中

$P_{\ell}(x) = \frac{1}{2^{\ell}\ell!}\frac{d^{\ell}}{dx^{\ell}}(x^{2} - 1)^{\ell} \quad (6.82)$

而 $P_{\ell}(x)$ 和 $P_{\ell}^{m}(x)$ ，分别称为 Legendre，和连带的 Legendre 多项式。因此

$Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta , \phi) = c\Theta_{\ell m}(\theta)e^{im\phi} = cP_{\ell}^{m}(\theta)e^{im\phi} \quad (6.83)$

$c$ 为待定之归一化常数。由归一化条件

$1 = \int_{0}^{2\pi}\int_{0}^{\pi}Y_{\ell}^{m*}(\theta,\phi)Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta,\phi)\sin\theta \, d\theta \, d\phi = 2\pi|c|^{2}\int_{0}^{\pi}P_{\ell}^{m*}(\theta)P_{\ell}^{m}(\theta)\sin\theta \, d\theta = 2\pi|c|^{2}\int_{-1}^{1}P_{\ell}^{m}(x)P_{\ell}^{m}(x)\, dx = |c|^{2}\frac{4\pi}{2\ell+1}\frac{(\ell+m)!}{(\ell-m)!}$

所以

$Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta, \varphi) = \sqrt{\frac{2\ell+1}{4\pi} \frac{(\ell-m)!}{(\ell+m)!}} P_{\ell}^{m}(\theta) e^{im\varphi} \quad (6.86)$

> [!definition] 球谐函数
> $Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta, \varphi) = \sqrt{\frac{2\ell+1}{4\pi} \frac{(\ell-m)!}{(\ell+m)!}} P_{\ell}^{m}(\theta) e^{im\varphi}$
>
> 称为球谐函数 (spherical harmonics)。它是 $\hat{L}^2$ 和 $\hat{L}_z$ 之本征态 $|\ell, m\rangle$ 在位置坐标 $(\theta, \varphi)$ 上之投影值，即
> $Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta, \varphi) = \langle \theta, \varphi | \ell, m \rangle \quad (6.87)$

一些常用的球谐函数 $Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta, \varphi)$ 为（参考附录）：

$$
\begin{align*} Y_0^0 &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{4\pi}}, \quad Y_1^0 = \sqrt{\frac{3}{4\pi}} \cos\theta = \sqrt{\frac{3}{4\pi}} \frac{z}{r}, \quad Y_1^{\pm 1} = \mp \sqrt{\frac{3}{8\pi}} e^{\pm i\varphi} \sin\theta = \mp \sqrt{\frac{3}{8\pi}} \frac{x \pm iy}{r} \\ Y_2^0 &= \sqrt{\frac{5}{16\pi}} (3\cos^2\theta - 1) = \sqrt{\frac{5}{16\pi}} \frac{2z^2 - x^2 - y^2}{r^2} \\ Y_2^{\pm 1} &= \mp \sqrt{\frac{15}{8\pi}} e^{\pm i\varphi} \cos\theta \sin\theta = \mp \sqrt{\frac{15}{8\pi}} \frac{(x \pm iy)z}{r^2} \\ Y_2^{\pm 2} &= \sqrt{\frac{15}{32\pi}} e^{\pm 2i\varphi} \sin^2\theta = \sqrt{\frac{15}{32\pi}} \frac{(x \pm iy)^2}{r^2} \end{align*} \quad (6.88)
$$

对轨道角动量的结果，现可结论于下：

满足轨道角动量算符 $\hat{L}^2$ 与 $\hat{L}_z$ 之本征值方程

$\hat{L}^2 \Psi_{\ell m} = \ell (\ell + 1) \hbar^2 \Psi_{\ell m} \quad \text{与} \quad \hat{L}_z \Psi_{\ell m} = m \hbar \Psi_{\ell m}$

其本征函数必为球谐函数

$\Psi_{\ell m}(\theta, \varphi) = Y_{\ell}^{m}(\theta, \varphi)$

且本征值 $\ell, m$ 均为整数。若 $\ell$ 已给定，则

$m = -\ell, -\ell+1, \dots 0, 1, 2, \dots \ell-1, \ell$

### 例6.1.（刚体转子的能谱）

有一刚体转子，长度为 $2a$ ，两端质量各为 $M$ 的粒子（如氢或氧分子 $H_{2}$ 或 $O_{2}$ ），求此转子的转动能量。

解由定义，转子绕其质量中心旋转的转动惯量与转动动能，分别为

$\mathrm{I} = 2M a^{2}, E = \frac{L^{2}}{2\mathrm{I}}$

故转子的转动能量算符

$\hat{H} = \frac{1}{2\mathrm{I}}\hat{L}^{2}$

![](Physics/QuantumMechanic/姚衍/images/Ch6-10_page4_0.jpg)

所观测到的能量值，由本征值方程 $\hat{H}\Psi = E\Psi$ 之本征值 $E$ 决定，即在解

$\frac{1}{2\mathrm{I}}\hat{L}^{2}\Psi = E\Psi \frac{\hbar^{2}}{\mathrm{I}}\ell$

但由式(6.65)已知 $\hat{L}^{2}$ 算符之本征值为 $\ell (\ell +1)\hbar^{2}$ 。

故转动能量为

$E = \frac{\ell(\ell + 1)\hbar^{2}}{2\mathrm{I}} = \frac{\ell(\ell + 1)}{4M a^{2}}\hbar^{2}$
